Bot Modes

Play against the computer. Pick a mode to start.

🧩 Puzzle 4 TYPES

Four kinds of puzzles: Win in 3, Race with a wall, Jump trick and Defend. Each position is set up for you and the bot plays back. Works offline.

⚡ Blitz 1 MIN + 2s

Classic 1v1 vs the bot with a 1 minute clock. Every move you make adds 2 seconds. If your time runs out, you lose.

🌫️ Fog of War NEW

The bot's walls stay hidden until they come near your pawn. Dark squares are out of sight. Walls you have seen are remembered as faint ghosts. Plan ahead, the bot may have blocked your road!

🧨 Obstacle Board NEW

Every game starts with random stones 🧨 you cannot walk through, and a pair of portals 🌀. Step on one portal and you pop out of the other. The layout is mirrored, so it is fair for both sides.

Dev PanelDEV ONLY

Developer tools. This page is only shown to developers.

🤖 Bot vs Bot Match WATCH

Pick two (or four) bots, their difficulty, pawn skin, animation and move style, choose the game mode, then watch the match on the normal game board. No points, gems or leaderboard changes.

🛡️ Match Control LIVE

See every running lobby and room. Reset or close a room, kick or ban players, manage the ban list. Includes the AI Guard moderator that flags AFK, bot-speed, rage-quit, collusion and reported players.

📢 Live Announcements ALL PLAYERS

Push a banner to every player’s screen, e.g. “Server update in 5 minutes” or “Dev Event started!”. Live countdown, 4 styles, multi-language. The AI Announcer drafts the message and reviews it before you send.

📊 Live Analytics REAL-TIME

How many players are online right now, who is in which room or mode, each player’s ping and gem income. The AI Analyst spots gem farming, lag spikes and player drops.

🖥️ Server Health MONITOR

Is the game server up? CPU, memory, response time, sockets and errors as live graphs. A status light stays in the corner of the Dev Panel. The AI Ops assistant warns about leaks, overload and cold starts.

🎁 Send Gems GIFT

Gift gems to any player by email, username or user ID, with an optional note. They get a gift popup on their screen the moment it arrives.
